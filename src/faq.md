Quick answers to the questions that come up again and again. If you don't see yours here, reach out any way you already have — or check the [Beginner Guide](/beginner-guide/) for the longer version of most of these.

## Getting Started

**Is wing foiling hard to learn?**

Harder than it looks in videos, easier than it looks from the beach once you've had a few sessions. Most people spend their first sessions just getting comfortable with the wing and the board separately before trying to put it all together on the foil. Expect a real learning curve, not an instant win — but it clicks faster than most people expect once the basics start to connect.

**Do I need to know how to kiteboard, windsurf, or surf first?**

No. Wing foiling is its own sport with its own learning curve. Experience in a related board sport can help with balance and water comfort, but plenty of people learn wing foiling as their first board sport entirely.

**What should I learn first — the wing or the foil?**

Most instructors recommend separating the skills: get comfortable handling the wing in the water (and maybe on a stand-up paddleboard or an easy board) before adding a hydrofoil into the mix. Trying to learn both at once multiplies the challenge. See the [Beginner Guide](/beginner-guide/) for the full progression.

**Should I take lessons?**

Yes — strongly recommended, especially for your first several sessions. This site is built from personal experience and researched information, not certified instruction. A qualified instructor can assess real conditions, correct your technique in real time, and keep you safe in ways a website can't. See the [Safety & Risk Disclaimer](/safety-risk-disclaimer/) for the full detail.

**How long does it take to get up on the foil?**

Highly variable — some people get a short ride within their first few sessions, others take longer. Prior board-sport experience, how often you get out, wind consistency, and the gear you're learning on all matter. Don't compare your timeline to someone else's.

## Gear

**What gear do I actually need to get started?**

A wing, a board, and a hydrofoil, sized to your weight and the wind you'll be riding in most often. Use the [gear calculator](/beginner-guide/what-gear-do-you-need/) for a personalized starting point rather than guessing.

**Should I buy new or used gear as a beginner?**

Used gear is a completely reasonable way to start, especially for your first setup while you're still figuring out what you like. Just have someone knowledgeable check foil and board condition before buying — hydrofoils in particular can be expensive to repair if damaged.

**What size wing do I need?**

Depends on your weight and the wind speed you're riding in — smaller wings for stronger wind, larger for light wind. Rough starting tiers: Light wind (10-15 mph) → ~5-6.5m², Moderate (15-20 mph) → ~4-5.5m², Strong (20-25 mph) → ~3-4.5m², Very strong (25+ mph) → ~2-3.5m². Use the [gear calculator](/beginner-guide/what-gear-do-you-need/) for a number tuned to you specifically.

**Do I need foot straps?**

Not to start — most beginner and intermediate wing foiling is strapless. Foot straps become worth considering once you're jumping seriously and want your feet to stay connected to the board during air time. See the [Jumping & Freestyle guide](/intermediate-advanced/jumping-and-freestyle/) for more.

**Where should I buy gear?**

See [Gear & Brands](/gear-and-brands/) for curated retailer links and a directory of major brands you can also shop directly.

## Safety & Wind

**What's the safest wind direction for a beginner?**

Side-shore to side-onshore wind is best — it gives you a self-rescue margin back toward shore without pushing you through breaking waves. Straight onshore wind isn't actually beginner-friendly despite blowing toward shore, since it can push you through surf and sandbars into the impact zone. Offshore wind (straight or angled) is the most dangerous condition at any skill level, since it blows you away from shore with no self-rescue option. Full detail on the [Wind page](/wind/).

**Is wing foiling dangerous?**

It's a real sport with real risk — wind, open water, a rigid board, and a hard hydrofoil moving fast under the surface. Collisions, falls, foil strikes, and getting caught in conditions beyond your ability are all real hazards. See the [Safety & Risk Disclaimer](/safety-risk-disclaimer/) for the full picture, and always assess actual on-water conditions yourself rather than relying solely on any website.

**What should I check before every session?**

Current wind speed and direction (not just the forecast — conditions change), that the forecast holds for your planned session length, that you're riding a spot suited to your skill level, and that someone knows where you are if you're heading out somewhere remote or unfamiliar.

**Do I need a leash?**

Yes, in almost all conditions — a board and foil moving on their own in wind and current is a real hazard to you and anyone else nearby. This isn't optional safety gear.

## Where to Ride & Community

**How do I find a good spot near me?**

Check the [Where to Ride map](/where-to-ride/) — it lists real spots with water type, best wind direction, and skill-level notes. Spots are marked as either firsthand-verified or community-reported, so you know how the information was sourced.

**Can I suggest a spot that's not on the map?**

Yes — use the Submit a Spot button on the [Where to Ride page](/where-to-ride/). Submissions are reviewed before anything gets added to the map.

**Can I submit my own story, trip report, or photos?**

That's coming soon — check back, or [sign up for the newsletter](/) to hear when it opens up.

**Is there a forum or comment section?**

Not currently, by design — we'd rather build a smaller number of genuinely useful pages than stand up a forum that feels empty early on. The best place to talk with other wing foilers right now is r/wingfoil and regional wing foil groups on Facebook and Instagram.

## About This Site

**Who runs Wing Foil America?**

An independent, personal passion project built to help people learn wing foiling — not a business, and not built or backed by any single gear brand. Read more on the [About page](/about/).

**Is this site sponsored by any brand?**

No. Gear and brand mentions reflect honest opinion and research, not paid placement. If that ever changes on a specific page (for example, an affiliate link), it'll be clearly disclosed on that page. See the [Terms of Use](/terms-of-use/) for the full policy.

**How often is new content added?**

Roughly one well-written page every week or two — this is a slow-and-steady project, not a content mill. [Sign up for the newsletter](/) to hear about new guides as they're published.

**I found something inaccurate — how do I let you know?**

Reach out any way you already have — a dedicated contact method is coming soon. Conditions, gear, and businesses change, so we appreciate corrections.
